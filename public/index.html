A free music sequencer with a retro heart.

ChipWave Studio is a complete music studio for Windows, macOS and Linux: lay clips out on a timeline, write melodies in the piano roll, program beats in the drum kit, and forge your own sounds with WaveForge, StringWave, FM-4, Analog and Chip FX. No subscription, no account, no limits.

New in 0.2.16.0

Your knobs, your words, your undo.

Three things that make the studio feel more like yours: controller maps you can keep, an Undo menu that says what it will undo, and a top display with a backlight glow and a glassy shine.

Midi

Save and load controller maps

Keep the knobs you have MIDI-learned as a small file for your keyboard or controller, then put them back whenever you like.

Workflow

Undo that names the step

The Edit menu reads Undo: Move clip or Redo: Add note for the most common edits, so you know before you click.

Look

A display that glows

The top LCD gets a soft backlight glow and a glassy shine in every display colour — Mint, Amber, Ice and the rest.

Features

Everything a song needs, in one window.

Arrange like a DAW, write like a sequencer, program like a tracker. ChipWave gives you the timeline, the notes, the drums, the sounds and the mix — with no project files to fight.

Arrange

Timeline and clip launcher

Drag clips across tracks, loop one clip with a double-click, split, copy and paste. Scenes in the Clip Arranger launch on the next bar, so you can try a whole arrangement without leaving the window.

  • Up to 32 tracks per song, each with its own colour and role
  • Loop, follow actions and Capture turn a jam into a timeline
  • Automation lanes for filters, levels, delay feedback and any knob
Write

Piano roll, drums and honest chords

Draw notes with velocity, pressure, chance and step conditions. The Drum Kit's step sequencer has swing, 16 to 64 steps and a groove generator, and the chord helper writes real notes to the timeline — one Ctrl+Z and it's yours.

  • Round Robin: clip variations that differ each pass but render the same
  • Chords, Melody and Bass generators with a WRITE card that says where the notes go
  • Scale lock for typing and MIDI, with key detection
Sound

A rack for every track

The Rack is the instrument and its effects in one row under the Timeline. Stack layers, split the keyboard into parallel paths, nest devices, save the result as your own rack and pull it back from the Browser.

  • Add Device from a tree, drag to reorder, Ctrl-drag to copy
  • Splits and layers with level, pan, transpose and key range
  • Save Custom Rack, then find it in Browser › My Racks
Mix

Mixer, ducking and a true-peak limiter

Per-channel fader, pan, mute, solo and peak meters, two send/return buses, and sidechain ducking with a Duck % per channel for the classic trance pump. The master limiter is a lookahead true-peak design, and the loudness meter reads LUFS the way the standards do.

  • Momentary, short-term and integrated LUFS plus true peak (ITU-R BS.1770)
  • Every export goes through the same limiter
  • WAV and MP3 with ID3 tags, whole song or one block
Classic

The Tracker is still there

For those who love the old way: OctaMED-style blocks, note and effect columns, the Z–M keyboard, selection, transpose and undo. Effects cover slides, decays, tempo, filter and acid lines; the cursor follows the playing row.

  • 64-row blocks by default, any length you like, with named Undo steps
  • 32 channels with a bar that says which ones you are looking at
  • 303 glide and accent (3C / 3D), plus Voice and generator effects
Find

A Browser that knows everything

Instruments, CLAP and VST3 plugins, samples, songs, presets, Grid patches and wavetables in one searchable panel with tags, filters, favourites and ratings. Drag a sound onto a track header to give that track the sound, or below the last track to make a new one.

  • Find plugins on the first run, or any time in Settings
  • Double-click to load, drag to assign, audition as you go
  • Chop and slice a break into instruments, or straight into notes
Plugins

CLAP and VST3, hosted properly

Surge XT and other CLAP instruments play from the timeline like any other instrument. Eight macro knobs are there for automation and 43xy, every plugin parameter is searchable, and the plugin state is saved inside the song.

  • Crash-guarded scanner, so one bad plugin does not take the app down
  • Native editor on Windows, offline render for the whole song
  • MIDI keyboards with velocity, sustain, pitch bend and MIDI learn
The sounds

Synths with character, made from scratch.

Every instrument is written for ChipWave itself: oscillators with drift, a Moog-style ladder, formant voices, FM and a pile of retro game noise. All of them are editable knob by knob, and all of them are in the free download.

WaveForge Synth

Two band-limited oscillators morphing pulse → saw → octave saw, up to 16 unison voices each, six blend modes, four filter types and eight mod routes.

StringWave Strings

A string ensemble ported in from its author's own synth: layered string machines with slow drift and a warm stereo spread.

Analog Synth

A Moog-style synth from scratch: three drifting oscillators, PWM, noise, the vintage ladder filter, filter and amp envelopes, with Fat Moog Bass and Acid Squelch presets.

FM-4 FM synth

Four operators, the eight DX21/TX81Z algorithms, feedback and an envelope per operator. E-Piano, Metallic Bell, Psy Zap Bass and Growl are ready to play.

Drum Kit & Drum Synth Beats

32 pads, 13 kits, choke groups, a step sequencer with velocity and swing, and a macro drum synth with Dice, Mutate and Bake to sample.

Chip FX Retro

A port of sfxr: pickups, lasers, explosions, power-ups, hits, jumps and blips that play in tune with the pattern note.

Voice Vocal

No samples: a glottal pulse through formant filters, vowels you build from chips, babble, stutter, spoken words, Throat mode and a metallic/digital/sub Transform.

Noise Texture

White, pink, brown and crackle through a ladder or state-variable filter with a sweep envelope. Risers, downlifters, impacts and vinyl.

Wave & Grid Wavetable

Wavetable and Grid patch instruments for when you want a sound that is not a subtractive synth at all.

Sampler Samples

Import WAV by button or drag and drop, loop, preview, and chop a break at its transients into slices or straight into notes.

Effects Mix

Filter, chorus/flanger, phaser, frequency shifter, delay, reverb, drive, bitcrusher, compressor, metal resonator, digital distortion and sub bass.

Plugins CLAP / VST3

Host CLAP and VST3 instruments and effects alongside the built-in ones, with macros, automation and saved plugin state.

Three looks

Slate, Dark Circuit, or a workbench from 1986.

Three themes, switched in View › Theme or Settings › Look. Slate is the default — the Ember skin on slate grey. Dark Circuit is the night-time studio. Amiga is the light Workbench look with blue headers and an orange cursor, for people who miss their A500.

Every theme keeps the same layout, keys and shortcuts, so switching never moves anything. There is also a UI scale in 25 % steps (Ctrl+= / Ctrl+-) and a text size setting for tired eyes and small laptops.

Download

Free, and free of strings.

Version 0.2.16.0, for 64-bit Windows, Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. Linux builds from source. The same download links always fetch the newest release.

Windows

64-bit · installer · 8.3 MB

Download installer Or the portable ZIP, 11.1 MB. Not code-signed yet: if SmartScreen appears, choose “More info” then “Run anyway”.

macOS

Universal · Apple Silicon + Intel · 25.2 MB

Download DMG macOS 11 or later, not notarised: first launch with Control-click → Open. Or the ZIP, 23 MB.

Linux

Build from source · Cargo

Build instructions Needs libasound2-dev. Then cargo run --release.

First run

The app opens with a clean song. To hear what it can do, open File › Demo songs and press Space.

SmartScreen

The Windows exe is not code-signed yet, so Windows may warn. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

macOS first launch

Control-click the app and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ChipWave.app. See MACOS.md.

Updating

On 0.2.3.5 or later, Help › Check for updates… offers the new version, or download it here again.
